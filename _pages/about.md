Hey there!

I am a third-year PhD student at the Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur in Nice, France, supervised by Dr. Héloïse Méheut and Prof. André Ferrari. My doctoral research, titled “Data-driven Approach for Planet-forming Disks Numerical Simulation”, focuses on combining data-driven methods with fluid dynamics simulations to model turbulence in planet-forming disks. Specifically, I am developing a hybrid neural network-solver framework that integrates machine learning with differentiable physics-based simulations. I am currently applying this framework to investigate gas–dust dynamics and their implications for planet formation.

Originally from Hong Kong, I completed my undergraduate and Master’s studies at the University of Cambridge, where I studied Natural Sciences before specialising in astrophysics. During my studies, I developed a strong interest in physics simulations and machine learning, which continues to fuel my curiousity about how the two can be combined to drive scientific discovery.

Outside of research, I enjoy outdoor sports and spending time in nature. On quieter days, I find joy in exploring the world through books, movies, art and music.

Feel free to reach out for a chat, whether it’s about research, potential collaborations, or simply shared interests!

Some of my other research interests:

* AI for physical systems
* Machine learning for weather and climate modelling
* Generative models
