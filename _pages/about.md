Hey there! 

I am a first-year PhD student at the Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur, based in the beautiful city of Nice, France. Supervised by Dr. Héloïse Méheut and Prof. André Ferrari, I am working on a project titled “Data-driven Approach for Planet-forming Disks Numerical Simulation”. My research explores how deep learning techniques can accelerate hydrodynamic simulations of protoplanetary disks, with a particular focus on modelling rotational turbulence and gas-dust dynamics that shape planet formation.

Originally from Hong Kong, I completed my Bachlor’s and Master’s degrees at the University of Cambridge, studying Natural Sciences and later specialising in astrophysics. During my studies, I developed a strong interest in physical simulations and machine learning, which now fuels my curiousity about how the two can be combined to drive discovery.

Outside of research, I enjoy outdoor sports. But on quieter days, I find joy in exploring the world through books, movies, art and music.

Feel free to reach out for a chat, whether it is research-related or just about shared interests!
